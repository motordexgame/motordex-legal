WRONG BIRTHDAY IN MOTORDEX?

MOTORDEX · BY MOTORDEX GAME LLC
FOR THE IPHONE AND ANDROID APPS · UPDATED 28 OCT 2026

MotorDex asks for a birth month and year before anything else. If the answer is under the minimum age, MotorDex stays locked on that phone, even after reinstalling. If the birthday was typed wrong, this is how to fix it.

HOW TO UNLOCK IT ON IPHONE

  • On the iPhone where MotorDex is locked, open MotorDex and tap CHECK WITH YOUR APPLE ACCOUNT.
  • Apple shares the age range of the Apple Account signed in on that iPhone: the age given when that Apple Account was made, or the age a parent set in Family Sharing. If it meets MotorDex’s minimum age, MotorDex unlocks and asks the birthday again. Enter it carefully: if it doesn’t match what Apple shared, MotorDex locks again on that iPhone for good.
  • If it still says “Your Apple Account couldn’t confirm your age,” check the birthday on the Apple Account signed in on that iPhone (for a child in Family Sharing, a parent can see it in Settings → [your name] → Family → [child]).
  • An adult’s Apple Account on an iPhone with parental controls turned on can’t unlock MotorDex, unless a parent set that age in Family Sharing.
  • MotorDex is for ages 13 and up (higher in some countries). If your child is under the minimum, we can’t make an exception, but we will delete their account and data on request.

HOW TO UNLOCK IT ON ANDROID

  • On the Android phone where MotorDex is locked, open MotorDex and tap CHECK WITH YOUR GOOGLE ACCOUNT.
  • Google Play shares the age range of the Google account on that phone: the age given when that Google account was made, or the age a parent set for it. If it meets MotorDex’s minimum age, MotorDex unlocks and asks the birthday again. Enter it carefully: if it doesn’t match what Google shared, MotorDex locks again on that phone for good.
  • If it still says “Your Google account couldn’t confirm your age,” check the birthday on the Google account used for Google Play on that phone (for a child’s supervised account, a parent can see it in the Family Link app).

IN AUSTRALIA

Australian law sets a minimum age of 16, and a typed birthday is not enough there. With an Australian App Store or Google Play account, MotorDex opens only once your Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) confirms that you are 16 or older. If the App says “in Australia you must be 16 or older, and your Apple Account (or Google account) must confirm it,” allow MotorDex to see your age range when your phone asks, or tap CHECK WITH YOUR APPLE ACCOUNT / CHECK WITH YOUR GOOGLE ACCOUNT on the locked page. On iPhone this needs iOS 26 or later. Nothing about this check is sent to MotorDex.
