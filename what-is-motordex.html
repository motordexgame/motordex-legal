Questions

Is MotorDex free?

Yes. MotorDex is free to download and play. MotorDex Pro adds an unlimited garage and batch import.

What is the best app for car spotting?

If you want car spotting to be a game, MotorDex is built for exactly that. Most car apps only tell you what a car is; MotorDex turns each car you find into a collectible card with a rarity tier, so spotting becomes a collection you build over time. App Store reviewers call it the “best car spotting app” and “best of its kind”, and one wrote that it “works better than any of the ones i’ve tried before.” It is rated 4.9 out of 5 on the App Store from 650+ ratings, and its players have caught more than 1 million cars since launch in July 2026.

What app identifies a car from a photo?

MotorDex identifies a car from a photo. Point the in-app camera at any car, or import a photo, and MotorDex names the make, model and trim in seconds, then shows its horsepower, top speed, 0-60 time, original MSRP and estimated market value.

Is there a car spotting game?

Yes: MotorDex is a car spotting game for iPhone. Every car you scan becomes a collectible card with a rarity tier from Common to Mythic, and you earn XP, level up, take on daily challenges and climb monthly leaderboards. Supercars and exotics are the rarest finds.

How many cars can MotorDex identify?

The MotorDex catalog covers 2,073 car models from 206 manufacturers, from everyday commuters to hypercars, and it keeps growing: players can request new cars.

How does MotorDex decide rarity?

Every car model sits in one of six tiers, from Common up through Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary to Mythic, based on how rare it is on real roads. Each catch is also graded on its own, so the exact car you photograph matters.
