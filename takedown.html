REQUEST REMOVAL OF AN INTIMATE IMAGE

MOTORDEX · BY MOTORDEX GAME LLC
NOTICE AND REMOVAL UNDER THE US TAKE IT DOWN ACT · UPDATED 20 OCT 2026

If an intimate image of you has been shared in MotorDex without your consent, you can ask us to remove it. This includes an image that was edited or generated by AI to look like you. We remove qualifying images within 48 hours of a valid request. This page says who can ask and what to send.

SEND YOUR REQUEST TO

support@motordexgame.com

Put TAKEDOWN in the subject line so it is seen at once.

WHO CAN ASK

  • The person shown in the image.
  • Someone authorised to act for that person: for example a parent or guardian of a minor, or a legal representative.

WHAT TO SEND

Your email needs these four things. Plain words are fine; there is no form to fill in.

  1. Your signature. Your typed full name counts as an electronic signature. If you are acting for the person shown, say so and give both names.
  2. Which image, and where it is. Enough for us to find it: the handle of the player who posted it and where it appears (a catch in the feed, a card, a profile photo). A screenshot of where it appears helps. You do not need to send the image itself.
  3. A short statement of good faith that the image was shared without your consent (or without the consent of the person you are acting for).
  4. Your contact details, so we can reach you: at least an email address.

WHAT WE DO

  • We remove a qualifying image within 48 hours of receiving a valid request, and we make reasonable efforts to find and remove identical copies.
  • We confirm the removal by email to the address you gave.
  • If something is missing from your request, we tell you what, so you can complete it.
  • We do not publish your request.
  • If the person shown is under 18, we also follow our child-safety process.

THE IN-APP REPORT TOOL

This process is in addition to the in-app Report tool. On any catch in the feed you can open its menu and tap REPORT THIS PHOTO; on the World leaderboard and in your friends list you can REPORT a name or BLOCK a player. For an intimate-image removal, email is the surest route, because it lets you give us the details above and lets us confirm back to you.

This page describes our notice-and-removal process. It is not legal advice. Other options, such as reporting to the police, remain open to you.
