DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

In the app: HOME → the gear in the top-right corner → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT, then confirm. Uninstalled the app, or can't sign in? Ask us by email instead; you do not need to reinstall anything.

Deleting removes your account, your catches and their photos for good. A few records, such as purchase records, are kept. Our Delete Your Account page lists exactly what is deleted and what we keep, and for how long.

Paying for Pro? Cancel the subscription first. Deleting your account does not stop the billing. How to cancel

Used Sign in with Apple? Check that MotorDex is gone from iPhone Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, and remove it there if it is still listed.

MOTORDEX PRO

MotorDex is free to play, and your garage holds 100 cards. Pro removes the card cap and adds batch import, burst mode and an extra AI double-check on your toughest finds: classics, lookalikes, deep cuts. Prices are shown in the app before you pay.

  • iPhone — a monthly or yearly subscription, bought through the App Store. Open it from the MOTORDEX PRO banner on HOME, or from Settings → ACCOUNT → UPGRADE TO PRO.
  • Android — Pro on Android is coming soon. It will be a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play. There is no Lifetime option on Android.

CANCEL A SUBSCRIPTION

A subscription belongs to your Apple or Google account, not to MotorDex, so cancel it in the store you bought it from, even if you now play on a different phone:

  • iPhone — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MotorDex → Cancel
  • Android — Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MotorDex → Cancel

After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not stop the billing. An iPhone Lifetime unlock (no longer sold; everyone who bought it keeps Pro) is not a subscription: there is nothing to cancel and nothing more to pay, and RESTORE PURCHASES on your iPhone brings it back on a new phone.

Refunds on iPhone are handled by Apple, not by us: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, you can ask Google Play (see Google Play's refund help) or email us and we will help.

RESTORE A PURCHASE

Pro is tied to your MotorDex account. On iPhone, sign in to the account you bought it on, open Pro the same way as above and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. A guest account can't restore, so sign in first. Still missing? Email us your handle and the date you bought it.

SCANNING

  • A scan failed or timed out. Scanning needs a network connection and a clear, well-lit shot of the car. Just try again.
  • Wrong car? A closer, less-angled photo usually fixes it. Right after a catch, tap WRONG CAR? TRY AGAIN under the card to pick from the AI's other guesses.
  • Daily scan limit. There is a generous per-day scan limit to keep the service healthy. Normal play won't hit it.

REPORT & BLOCK

In the friends feed you can report a catch or block a player. Blocking removes them from your feed and your friends.
