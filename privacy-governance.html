HOW WE LOOK AFTER YOUR INFORMATION

PRIVACY GOVERNANCE AND COMPLAINTS · MOTORDEX GAME LLC
UPDATED 28 OCT 2026

This page sums up, in plain words, the rules we follow inside MotorDex to protect personal information, and how to complain if you are not happy. Our Privacy Policy says what we collect and why.

WHO IS IN CHARGE

Our Privacy Officer is the Owner and Managing Member of MotorDex Game LLC. He is the person in charge of the protection of personal information (including under Québec law). He decides who may see players' data, and access is limited to what each task needs. The companies that host and process data for us may use it only on our instructions, under written agreements.

OUR RULES

  • We collect only what the game needs, and say why before we collect it.
  • We use information only for the reasons in the Privacy Policy.
  • Before we build or change a system that uses personal information, we check the privacy risks first.
  • Before information leaves Québec (our servers are in the United States), we check it will be properly protected and sign a written agreement.
  • Every kind of information has a time limit. When it ends, the information is deleted or made anonymous automatically. The list is in Section 8 of the Privacy Policy.
  • If information is lost or exposed, we record it, and we tell the regulator and the people affected when the law requires.

SEE, FIX OR DELETE YOUR INFORMATION

In the app: SETTINGS → PRIVACY lets you download your data, delete your account, turn AI scanning off and remove locations. Or email us. We answer within 30 days.

HOW TO COMPLAIN

  1. Email support@motordexgame.com with PRIVACY in the subject, or write to the address above. Anyone can complain. A parent can complain for a child.
  2. We confirm we got it within 5 business days.
  3. We send you a written answer within 30 days.
  4. If you are not satisfied, you can go to a regulator: in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI); elsewhere in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada; in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your country's data protection authority; in Switzerland, the FDPIC.

NOS RÈGLES

  • Nous recueillons seulement ce dont le jeu a besoin, et nous disons pourquoi avant de le recueillir.
  • Nous utilisons les renseignements seulement pour les raisons indiquées dans la Politique de confidentialité.
  • Avant de créer ou de modifier un système qui utilise des renseignements personnels, nous évaluons d'abord les risques pour la vie privée.
  • Avant que des renseignements quittent le Québec (nos serveurs sont aux États-Unis), nous vérifions qu'ils seront bien protégés et nous signons une entente écrite.
  • Chaque type de renseignement a une durée de conservation. À la fin, il est détruit ou anonymisé automatiquement. La liste est à la section 8 de la Politique de confidentialité.
  • Si des renseignements sont perdus ou exposés, nous l'inscrivons dans un registre et nous avisons la Commission et les personnes concernées lorsque la loi l'exige.
