Anyone can use this page, with or without a MotorDex account. Use it to tell us about content in MotorDex that you believe is illegal, to complain about how we keep people safe, or to ask us to review a decision we made about your content or account. A person reads every submission.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the police now (112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US). For child sexual abuse material you can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline or your national hotline. Do not download, copy or share the material, even to report it.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

  1. We confirm receipt by email straight away, with a reference number (for example MD-1A2B3C4D5E). We never repeat your words in our emails.
  2. A person reviews it. Reports of a child at risk, a threat to someone's life or safety, self-harm or suicide, terrorist content or an intimate image shared without consent are looked at first. We check whether the content is illegal or breaks our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines. Our automated screening may already have checked the content, but on this form a person makes the decision.
  3. We act where needed: we may remove or hide the photo, remove a profile photo, change a username, or suspend or close the account. Where the law requires it we preserve the material and report it to the authorities, including when a life may be at risk.
  4. We email you our decision, the reason for it, whether any automated means were used, and what you can do if you disagree. Complaints are answered the same way; we aim to reply within 14 days.

The player concerned is told what we did and why, with how to appeal, in the app. We do not tell them who sent the notice, unless the law requires it or it is strictly necessary (for example, a rights holder's name in a copyright claim).

IF YOU DISAGREE

  • Ask us to review it. Reply to our decision email, or use this form and choose REVIEW A DECISION, within 6 months. A person — where possible one who was not involved in the first decision — looks at it again and tells you the outcome. It is free.
  • In the European Union you may also take the dispute to an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under Article 21 of the Digital Services Act. The European Commission publishes the list of certified bodies.
  • You can always go to court. None of these routes takes away your right to do so.

WHAT WE KEEP

We keep what you send — including your name and email — only to handle it: to review it, act on it, reply to you and defend our decision. It is deleted 12 months after our decision, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example a legal hold or a report to the authorities). We do not store your network (IP) address: to stop abuse we keep a scrambled code of it that changes every day and is erased after 2 days. Our Privacy Policy explains your rights.
