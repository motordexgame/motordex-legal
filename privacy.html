Effective October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MotorDex Game LLC (“MotorDex,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, retains, and otherwise processes information when you use the MotorDex mobile application (the “App”), visit motordexgame.com (the “Website”), join our waitlist, contact us, or otherwise interact with our services (collectively, the “Services”). Please read it carefully. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the Services.

MotorDex is a game that lets you photograph real cars, use automated tools to identify them, and collect the resulting cars as digital Catch Cards. We use information to operate the game and its social features. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Some features are optional, and you can control certain collection through the App and your device settings.

For younger players, in short: You must be 13 or older (older in some countries). We keep your birth month and year private. Map pins are off (players aged 16 or 17 can turn them on), and even then we keep only a rough area. You are hidden from the World leaderboard unless you switch yourself on. Only friends you add by code see your catches, never where you took them. An AI checks photos and names to keep everyone safe. Ask a parent before buying Pro. You can download or delete your data in Settings → PRIVACY.

4. Photos, Artificial Intelligence, and Model Training

4.1 Vehicle identification and safety screening

If you play as a guest, your photos are not sent to our AI provider until you create an account. When you submit a photo, the image is sent through our server to our AI provider (named in Section 7.1) to identify the vehicle and to run an automated image-safety check. The safety check is designed to screen for objectionable imagery, including nudity or sexual content, graphic violence, and hate imagery. The same kind of automated check is applied to optional profile photos. For players who have given AI consent, we also use the same provider to check handles for offensive names, to check other versions of your catch photos saved since October 18, 2026 (the full-size photo, thumbnail, and any re-framed version) before your friends can see them, to pre-sort reported photos (Section 6), and to re-check existing handles periodically. We send only the photo itself and our fixed instructions. The photo’s hidden data (location, camera, date and other metadata) is removed on your phone and again on our server before the image leaves us. For handle checks we send the handle text only. We do not send your location, email or account ID. Each request may carry a scrambled code in their place, which is pseudonymous: it is derived from your account with a secret key and changes every day, so we can match it to an account if the provider reports abuse. Our instructions tell the AI never to read license plates, vehicle identification numbers or anything about people, and any badge or other text it reads is not logged or stored, except the car’s make, model and trim shown on your Catch Card. Our server also removes any plate- or VIN-like text from the AI’s answers before they are stored or shown. Every request also carries child-safety instructions. Scanning requires an age answer and your AI consent to be on record. An upload that our screening flags as inappropriate is deleted or hidden from other players, normally right away. When a photo is reported, the provider may pre-sort it (never for reports made under our child-safety or intimate-image reasons): a high-confidence sexual or child-safety result hides the photo from your friends until a person reviews it. Automated screening is a safeguard, not a guarantee that all prohibited or objectionable content will be detected. Images that pass screening may be stored and displayed as part of your Catch Card, collection, and permitted social features.

Our AI provider processes the image to provide these API functions under the terms applicable to our use of the service. The provider’s processing of the image is governed by the API and privacy terms applicable to the service. MotorDex’s practices do not replace the provider’s own applicable terms. See Section 7 for more information. Under its current terms, the provider processes the content we send it on our behalf, under its Data Processing Addendum, and by default does not use it to train its models. It may keep our inputs and its outputs for up to 30 days. Content flagged by its trust and safety systems may be kept for up to 2 years and safety classifier scores for up to 7 years, even under zero data retention, which we have requested. AI processing happens in the United States. The provider’s safety review of flagged content may take place outside the United States. Our AI provider (named in Section 7.1) states that it checks images on its services against known child-sexual-abuse hash lists and reports matches to NCMEC. If we learn of apparent child sexual abuse material, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and preserve the related material we hold for at least one year, as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A.

5. Location Information

Location is optional. If you grant the App location permission while using it, the App may associate the location at the time of a catch with that catch so it can appear on your personal map. The location stored with a catch is rounded to approximately 110 metres (about 2 km for players under 18, whose exact location stays only on their device). Some older catches keep a more precise location. We do not collect background location. Precise-location capture is disabled for users who indicate that they are under 16. For players under 18, location pins are off by default. A player aged 16 or 17 may turn on PIN MY CATCHES ON THE MAP in Settings (for players under 16, precise-location capture stays off and pins cannot be turned on). A sign then shows on the SCAN screen while a location may be taken. The exact location stays on the player’s phone. Our server stores only an approximate area of about 2 km. The exact location on the phone is not backed up, so after a new phone or a reinstall older catches appear at the 2 km point. We can switch pins off for all players under 18 from our server. For adults, pins are on by default (off by default with a Canadian app-store account, see Section 12) and can be switched off in Settings. With pins off, no location is read or sent for new catches. If your phone shares only an approximate location (iPhone Precise Location off, or Android “Approximate”), the location saved in an imported photo is also reduced to about 2 km before it is kept, shown or sent. You can also remove the location from any single catch. It then comes off your map for good. If you deny or revoke location permission, the App remains usable, but new catches may not have map pins. You can change permission at any time in your device settings.

Your catch locations are not shared with friends or displayed on the World leaderboard. Before a friend views your collection, location information is removed from the data supplied to that friend. Location is used to provide the map feature you choose to use, not to infer personal characteristics about you, and is not sold or shared for advertising.

The App uses Apple MapKit on iOS and the Google Maps SDK on Android. On Android, when you use the map, the Google Maps SDK sends Google information such as your IP address, map interactions, crash data, and a pseudonymous identifier. These map providers may process information under their own privacy policies when map features are used. Review the relevant provider’s policy for details about its independent processing.
