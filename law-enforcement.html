EMERGENCY REQUESTS

If you believe someone faces an imminent risk of death or serious physical injury, we may disclose information without legal process (18 U.S.C. §§ 2702(b)(8) and 2702(c)(4)). Send an EMERGENCY DISCLOSURE REQUEST from an official address describing the emergency, why it is imminent, the account involved (the player's handle as shown in the app), and the specific information you need. We read these requests as a priority. We may ask for confirmation through your agency.

PRESERVATION REQUESTS

We honour preservation requests under 18 U.S.C. § 2703(f). On a valid request we take a copy of the account's records and keep it for 90 days, extendable once by a further 90 days on a renewed request, while you obtain legal process. Identify the account by handle (and, if you have them, the date of a catch or a screenshot of the profile) and say what you want preserved.

Child sexual abuse material we become aware of is reported to NCMEC's CyberTipline and preserved for one year as federal law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A).

LEGAL PROCESS WE REQUIRE

  • Basic subscriber information (handle, email address if any, sign-in method, account creation date, recent sign-in IP addresses): a valid subpoena or equivalent process (18 U.S.C. § 2703(c)(2)).
  • Other non-content records (such as catch timestamps and the approximate location stored with a catch): a court order under 18 U.S.C. § 2703(d).
  • Content (photos, notes): a search warrant (18 U.S.C. § 2703(a)-(b)).

Requests must be specific: the account (handle), the date range, and the data sought. We may push back on requests that are overly broad, unclear, or not legally valid. We do not charge for responding to valid requests.

WHAT MOTORDEX HOLDS

MotorDex is a car-spotting game. Players photograph cars and collect them as cards. There are no private messages. What an account can have:

  • Account: handle, an email address (only for email sign-ups; Sign in with Apple often gives a private relay address; guest accounts have none), sign-in method, creation date and, where collected, an age bracket and records of the terms and AI consents accepted.
  • Sign-in records: IP address and device type of sign-in sessions, kept for 30 days after the session was last used.
  • Catches: the photo of each car, the time it was taken, and the location if the player allowed it (current versions round it on our server to an area of about 110 m; some older catches hold a more precise location). Kept until the player deletes the catch or the account.
  • Profile photo, friends list (by handle) and reactions.
  • Reports and moderation records: kept for one year after a case is closed, including after the account is deleted.
  • Purchases: records of App Store / Google Play subscriptions as reported by the stores. We never receive card numbers.

When a player deletes their account, their data is removed and cannot be recovered by us, except the records our Privacy Policy says we keep (backups roll off on the schedule stated there). Retention periods are set out in full in the Privacy Policy.

NOTICE TO USERS

Our policy is to tell a player before disclosing their information, so they can seek legal advice, unless the law forbids it (for example a non-disclosure order under 18 U.S.C. § 2705(b)), the request is an emergency, or notice would put someone at risk or be counterproductive (for example in child-safety cases).

REQUESTS FROM OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES

MotorDex Game LLC is a US company. Non-US authorities should use a Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT), a letters rogatory process, or an agreement under the US CLOUD Act where one applies. Emergency requests from any country are reviewed as above.
