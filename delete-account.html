MOTORDEX · BY MOTORDEX GAME LLC
FOR THE IPHONE AND ANDROID APPS · UPDATED 18 OCT 2026

This page is for MotorDex, the car spotting app for iPhone and Android made by MotorDex Game LLC.

You can delete your MotorDex account, and the data stored with it, at any time and for any reason. There are two ways. Neither needs a reason, and the email route works even if you no longer have the app.

Paying for a Pro subscription, monthly or yearly? Cancel it first. Deleting your account does not stop the billing. How to cancel

1 — IN THE APP

Instant, and it works for guest accounts too.

  • Open MotorDex and go to HOME.
  • Tap the gear in the top-right corner to open Settings.
  • Open the ACCOUNT tab.
  • Tap DELETE ACCOUNT, then confirm. On Android, tap it again when it says CONFIRM — DELETE?. On iPhone, tap DELETE FOREVER.

Your account is deleted straight away and the app goes back to its first screen. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to disconnect MotorDex. To check, or to do it yourself: iPhone Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → MotorDex.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Delete it in the app first, or email us.

2 — BY EMAIL

Use this if you have uninstalled MotorDex, lost your phone, or cannot sign in. You do not need to reinstall anything.

EMAIL US

support@motordexgame.com

Use the subject line Account deletion request and tell us your handle. So we know the account is yours:

  • Email account: send it from the email address you sign in with.
  • Sign in with Apple: send it from any address. We will write to the address Apple gave us for your account, and you confirm by replying to that email.
  • Guest (no email): while the app is still on your phone, delete the account in the app (steps above). A guest account has no email or name, so we cannot check by email that it is yours, and once the app is uninstalled nobody can sign in to it again, you included. Email us your handle anyway and we will explain what we can do.

We delete the account within 7 days and email you when it is done. We will never ask for your password or a reset code.

WHAT IS DELETED

Whichever way you delete, this is removed for good, and we cannot get it back. In the app it happens straight away; by email, within 7 days.

  • Your sign-in: your email address, password, Sign in with Apple link, and your sign-in sessions (including the IP address each one came from)
  • Your handle, Spotter License and profile photo
  • Every catch: the cards, every photo you scanned (including the full-size copies), their map locations and your private notes
  • Dex progress, XP, level, badges, daily challenges and streaks
  • Friends, friend requests, reactions and blocks
  • Reports you made, reports other players made about you, wrong-car reports, car requests and promo codes you tried
  • The log of every scan you made
  • Your place on the leaderboards, past months included (the public boards update within a few minutes)
  • Pro on this account (see Cancel Pro first)

Card images you shared outside the app, for example to a chat or to social media, are copies we cannot reach.

WHAT WE KEEP

A few records are not stored with your profile, so deleting the account does not remove them. None of them holds your photos or your catch locations. Where one keeps your account number, it is the random ID the app gave you; once the account is deleted and the short-lived server logs below have expired, nothing in our systems links that ID to your email address.

  • Purchase records, if you ever bought Pro. What you bought, the price, currency, country, date and the store's transaction ID, with your old account number. We keep a copy, so does RevenueCat (the service that handles our in-app purchases), and Apple or Google keep their own under their rules. HOW LONG: AS LONG AS TAX AND ACCOUNTING LAW REQUIRES
  • A few service records, with your old account number. How many scans the account made each day (for the daily scan limit); when you used TRY AGAIN, which car the AI guessed first and which car you chose (to make scanning more accurate; the photo itself is deleted); any feedback you typed in Settings; and your handle with our automatic name check's verdict on it. HOW LONG: NO SET END DATE YET. DELETE BY EMAIL AND WE REMOVE THESE TOO
  • Backups and server logs. Copies of deleted data can stay in our encrypted database backups and in server logs for a short time, then expire on their own. HOW LONG: UP TO 30 DAYS
  • Your email to us. If you ask us by email, we keep that conversation as a record that we did what you asked. HOW LONG: ONLY AS LONG AS WE NEED TO SHOW THE REQUEST WAS COMPLETED

We do not sell any of this or use it for advertising. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.

KEEP THE ACCOUNT, DELETE SOME DATA

  • One catch: open the card, tap the trash icon, then DELETE?. The card goes at once. Its photos leave our storage within about 8 days, except a photo you used with TRY AGAIN, which stays until you delete the account.
  • Every catch at once: Settings → ACCOUNT → RESET, then confirm. Your account, handle and friends stay. The photos leave our storage the same way.
  • Your profile photo: Settings → PROFILE → LICENSE PHOTO → USE MY CAR. The photo is deleted from our servers straight away.
  • Location: turn location off for MotorDex in your phone's settings and new catches you take with the camera get no map pin. A photo you add from your gallery is pinned at the location saved in it only if you allowed that (on Android: Settings → APP → PIN GALLERY CATCHES, ON or OFF). A pin can't be removed on its own: to remove one, delete that catch.

CANCEL PRO FIRST

If you pay for MotorDex Pro monthly or yearly, cancel the subscription BEFORE you delete your account. Deleting the account does not stop the billing. The app store charges you, not us, and it keeps charging whether or not the account still exists.

You do not need your MotorDex account to cancel. The subscription belongs to your Google or Apple account, so this still works after the app is gone. It is just easier to remember while the app is still on your phone.

  • Android — Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MotorDex → Cancel
  • iPhone — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MotorDex → Cancel

A one-time Lifetime unlock is not a subscription. There is nothing to cancel and nothing more to pay. Deleting the account removes Pro from it; if you bought Lifetime and want it on a new account later, email us.

Already deleted your account and still being charged? Email us at the address above and we will help you sort it out.
