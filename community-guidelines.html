COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

MOTORDEX · BY MOTORDEX GAME LLC
FOR THE IPHONE AND ANDROID APPS · UPDATED 28 OCT 2026

MotorDex is about cars. Your photos, your profile photo and your handle (player name) can be seen by other players, so a few rules keep the game safe for everyone. They apply to every player, and they are part of our Terms of Use.

THE RULES WHAT WE DO REPORT & BLOCK APPEALS

THE RULES

  • Photograph cars, not people. Keep people out of the frame. No faces, no children, no one who has not agreed to be photographed.
  • No plates or private information. Do not post number plates, home addresses, names, or anything that identifies a person, whether in a photo, a profile photo or a handle.
  • No nudity or sexual content. In photos, profile photos or handles. Anything sexual involving a minor is reported to the authorities. See Child Safety.
  • No violence, hate or harassment. No violent or graphic images, no slurs, no content that attacks people for who they are, and no bullying of other players.
  • Nothing that could harm a young player. No content that promotes, encourages or explains suicide, self-harm or eating disorders; nothing that encourages drugs, alcohol, tobacco, vaping or other harmful substances; and no dangerous stunts or challenges, including dangerous driving. In photos, profile photos or handles. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or contact a local helpline.
  • No impersonation. Do not pose as another person, a company or a car brand, in a handle or a profile photo.
  • Your own photos only. Upload photos you took yourself. No press shots, screenshots, stock images or other people's pictures. See Copyright Notices.
  • Stay safe and legal. Never scan while driving. Do not trespass, and respect private property and no-photography areas.
  • Play fair. No scripts, no fake catches, no cheating.

WHAT WE DO

Every photo and handle is screened automatically before it can appear. Our staff review reports. When something breaks these rules we may:

  • remove the photo, profile photo or other content;
  • rename an offensive handle;
  • suspend or close the account. Repeat offenders lose the account.

If three of your uploads are rejected by the automatic check within 24 hours, scanning and photo uploads stop for 24 hours.

A machine can only sort and hide things for a while; a person decides removals, renames and suspensions. When we do something to your photo, name or account, the app shows you a notice: what we did, which rule, how we found it (a report, our automatic check or our own review), whether a machine or a person decided, and how to disagree.

Where the law requires, we also preserve material and report it to the authorities.

HOW TO REPORT AND BLOCK

  • Report a photo: on any catch in the feed, open its menu and tap REPORT THIS PHOTO.
  • Block a player: from the same menu, tap BLOCK. Blocking removes that player and their content from your feed and your friends.
  • Report a name: on the World leaderboard and in your friends list, tap REPORT next to the handle, or BLOCK.
  • By email: write to support@motordexgame.com with the handle and what you saw. For a concern about a child, put CHILD SAFETY in the subject; to have an intimate image of you removed, see Request removal of an intimate image.
  • Not a player, or reporting something illegal? Anyone can use our report and complaints form — no account needed. It is also where to complain (for example under the UK Online Safety Act) about how we keep people safe.

A person looks at every report. When we have decided, the app tells you what we did about what you reported (from version 1.0.8; on the form, we email you).

APPEALS

If we removed or hid your content, renamed your handle, paused your uploads or suspended your account — or you disagree with what we did about something you reported — and you think we got it wrong:

  • tap DISAGREE? TELL US on the notice in the app, or email us with APPEAL in the subject line, or use the form. Tell us your handle and what happened; the notice number helps. You have 6 months.
  • A person — where possible not the one who decided — looks at it again and replies by email, normally within 7 days. If we got it wrong, we undo it.
  • In the EU you can also go to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body, and anyone can go to court.
